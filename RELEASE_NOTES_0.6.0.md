## What's new in 0.6.0
- Battery level and solar power (W) sensors
- Daylight, wind input and connection binary sensors
- Repairs issue when the controller is unreachable for 3 minutes
- Timing hours / minutes number entities (disabled by default)
- Register 10 is the generation counter (no longer shown as a charge stage)

Ideas from [wescom-code/zhijinpower_ble](https://github.com/wescom-code/zhijinpower_ble), re-implemented. After updating, restart Home Assistant.
